# Privacy

Xyether HandBrake processes videos on your Android device. The app has no Internet permission, analytics, advertising SDK, account system, or cloud upload.

It reads videos you select through Android's pickers. When available, it retains permission to read those selected files so queued jobs can be reopened.

The queue, settings, file names, and private exports are stored inside the app. **Save to device** makes a separate copy in shared storage. **Share** grants the selected receiving app access to the exported video.

Deleting a history entry removes its private export, not a separate copy you already saved to Movies. Uninstalling removes private app data. Android backup behavior is controlled by the app manifest and your system settings.
